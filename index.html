Creator Outreach Studio is an independently developed software project that helps organize public creator research, manual qualification, and partnership outreach workflows.

Development and local testing · Not publicly available

What the software is designed to do

Video-based discovery

Find public YouTube videos and their publishing channels using product and topic searches.

Multiple discovery paths

Explore keyword, topic similarity, competitor-related, and product-use-case search plans.

Human-led review

Assess content relevance and publicly available business contact information before considering outreach.

Workflow organization

Maintain local candidate records, review statuses, and manual outreach history for organized follow-up.

Project status and API use

The application is being developed and tested locally by an independent developer. It uses the YouTube Data API Services to locate public video results and retrieve relevant public channel and video information to support creator discovery and review.

Creator candidates are reviewed by a human before outreach. The application is not a YouTube product, is not endorsed by YouTube or Google, and does not provide public access to creator databases.
